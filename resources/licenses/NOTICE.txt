This app bundles sing-box (Resources/bin/sing-box), an unmodified release
build of https://github.com/SagerNet/sing-box, which is free software under
the GNU General Public License v3 or later, with the additional term in
sing-box-LICENSE.txt. It runs as a separate program that this app starts and
controls through its local API.

The version bundled is the one named by SINGBOX_VERSION in
scripts/fetch-singbox.sh. Its complete corresponding source is at
https://github.com/SagerNet/sing-box (tag v<version>).

This app is not made by, and is not endorsed by, the sing-box authors.
